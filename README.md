# 📊 HR Employee Attrition Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Employee attrition is one of the biggest challenges organizations face. High employee turnover can increase recruitment costs, reduce productivity, and impact overall business performance.

This project analyzes HR employee data using Python and Pandas to identify key factors influencing employee attrition and provide actionable business insights.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The primary objective of this project is to understand:

* Why employees leave the organization
* Which employee groups are at higher risk of attrition
* The impact of salary, overtime, job satisfaction, and promotions on employee retention
* How organizations can reduce employee turnover

---

## 📂 Dataset Information

The dataset contains employee-related information such as:

* Age
* Gender
* Department
* Job Role
* Monthly Income
* Job Satisfaction
* Work Life Balance
* Environment Satisfaction
* Years at Company
* Total Working Years
* Years Since Last Promotion
* Overtime
* Attrition Status

## 💡 Key Insights

* Employees working overtime show higher attrition rates.
* Lower job satisfaction is associated with increased employee turnover.
* Employees with delayed promotions are more likely to leave.
* Work-life balance significantly impacts employee retention.
* Certain departments and job roles experience higher attrition than others.
* Salary and career growth opportunities play an important role in employee satisfaction.

---

## 📈 Business Recommendations

* Reduce excessive overtime workloads.
* Improve employee engagement programs.
* Create transparent promotion policies.
* Enhance work-life balance initiatives.
* Focus retention efforts on high-risk departments and job roles.
* Develop career growth opportunities for employees.
